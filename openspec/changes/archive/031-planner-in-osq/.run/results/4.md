## Deviated

- The task detail prescribes `.option('-print, --print', ...)`. Commander 13.1.0
  rejects multi-character single-dash short flags (`option creation failed due
  to '-print'`), so the committed implementation registers `-p, --print` and
  normalizes `-print` to `--print` in the `parse`/`parseAsync` overrides in
  `src/cli/index.ts`. This satisfies the functional contract
  `osq plan <name> -print` and was left unchanged. Verified end to end against a
  temp project: exit 0, prompt on stdout, nothing on stderr, no harness spawn.
- The command description in `src/cli/index.ts` is
  `'initialize change, write brief, and open interactive planner session'`
  rather than the detail's `'initialize and launch interactive planning session'`.
  Cosmetic only; acceptance lines do not depend on it.
- `spawnInteractive` remains an optional member of `HarnessAdapter`
  (`spawnInteractive?`) as established by task 2; `planCommand` guards its
  absence and throws a descriptive error.
