### Measuring

Use `ts.createSourceFile(name, text, ts.ScriptTarget.Latest, true)` and walk
every node with `ts.forEachChild`. Nested functions are measured too, under
their own names. For a `const`, measure the initializer and key it by the
variable's name. For declarations and methods, use `node.name.getText(sf)`
and skip nameless ones. Compute line numbers with
`sf.getLineAndCharacterOfPosition` on `node.getStart(sf)` and `node.getEnd()`.
Callbacks passed inline as arguments are not measured.
