## Changed

- `src/watcher/task-verify.ts` (new): owns the pre-spawn check. `runPreSpawnVerify` reads the attempt from `readRetryContext`, skips on `off` or attempt !== 1, runs the task verify through `runVerificationGateResult` with `extraData` adding `phase: "pre_spawn"`, `expected`, and `mismatch` computed by `isPreSpawnMismatch`, warns once under `warn`, and returns a `verify_precondition` marker/error under `fail`. Also holds `formatPreSpawnWarning`, `formatPreSpawnDeadMarker`, and `verifyRedFailure` (the post-spawn `verify_red` marker text, moved unchanged).
- `src/watcher/runner.ts`: one short `runPreSpawnVerify(verifyCtx)` call after `captureTestGate` and before `createTaskMeasures`, failing through the existing `fail` helper; the post-spawn verify_red marker text now delegates to `verifyRedFailure`.
- `src/watcher/verify.ts`: `runVerificationGateResult`'s context accepts an optional `extraData(result)` field that is spread into the `verify_ran` event data after the usual fields. Every other caller is unchanged.
- `src/harness/types.ts`: `VerifyRanEventData` gains optional `phase?: 'pre_spawn'`, `expected?: 'red' | 'green' | 'any'`, `mismatch?: boolean`.
- `src/watcher/outcome.ts`: `RunTaskFailureReason` gains `verify_precondition`.
- `tests/pre-spawn-verify.test.ts` (new): drives the real `runTask` on an approved temp change with local pass/fail/flip verifiers, a flag-writing adapter, and a warning-capturing logger. Covers red-expected-match to `done`, warn mismatch and its single warning, green pass/fail, `any`, `fail`-mode death with full marker/event evidence and no spawn, `off`, and an attempt-2 stream that skips the check.
- `tests/fixtures/events/verified.jsonl` and `dead.jsonl`: regenerated with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`; each gains only the leading pre-spawn `verify_ran` line (`mismatch: true` / `false`).

## Deviated

- The post-spawn `verify_red` marker and failure projection moved into `task-verify.ts` as `verifyRedFailure`, but the `runVerificationGate` call itself stays in `runTask` (the task said to move the marker text, not the gate). This keeps `runner.ts` at 197 lines.
- `verify.ts` keeps a one-line `VerifyEventDataFn` alias and drops the blank line above it so `source.split('\n').length` stays under the stricter check in `tests/runner-synthesized-result.test.ts` (which counts the trailing newline, unlike the import-graph budget).
