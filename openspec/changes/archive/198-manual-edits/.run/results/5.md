## Changed

- Added `src/core/report/manual-edits-section.ts`: exports `MANUAL_EDITS_HEADING`, `MANUAL_EDITS_INTRO`, `formatManualEditsSection(projectRoot, config)` (the body or null) and the small `appendManualEditsSection` used by the prompt builder. It reads records with `readManualEditRecords` and archived changes with `listArchivedChanges`, treats a capability as open when no archived change whose delta writes it archived later than the record's `kept_at`, and renders one line per record with an open capability in `kept_at` order.
- `src/cli/plan.ts`: `buildOpeningPrompt` now appends the `## Manual edits` section last, after `## Recent executor disclosures`, and leaves it out when the body is null.
- Added `tests/plan-manual-edits.test.ts` with two plain `node:test` blocks named after the scenarios: `Open manual edits` proves the body through `formatManualEditsSection` over a temporary project with three record files and one archived change; `No open manual edits` compares `buildOpeningPrompt` with and without records whose capabilities a later archived change wrote, asserting byte equality and no heading.

## Deviated

- Added `appendManualEditsSection` rather than importing `MANUAL_EDITS_HEADING` and appending inline in `plan.ts`: `src/cli/plan.ts` was at 249 of the 250-line budget, and this keeps the added prompt code to one line plus one import. The heading and body formatter are still exported as the acceptance line requires.
- Trimmed one prose line from `buildOpeningPrompt`'s doc comment (its meaning and every `@scenario`/`@adr` tag are kept, plus the new `Manual edits in the plan prompt` tag) to stay inside the budget: `src/cli/plan.ts` is now exactly 250 lines and `tests/line-budget.test.ts` passes.
