## Changed

- `src/core/report/query-manual-edits.ts` (new): `MANUAL_EDIT_TABLES`, the
  `manual_edits(kept_at, record, comment, paths, capabilities)` table, and
  `insertManualEdits(db, projectRoot, config)`, which fills it from
  `readManualEditRecords`, one row per record in `kept_at` order. `record` is
  the record's file name, `comment` its whole comment, and `paths` and
  `capabilities` its lists joined with `, `, an empty list reading as the
  empty string. The function carries the three `@scenario metrics-and-reporting`
  lines of "Manual edits query table" and `@adr 008`.

  `insertManualEdits`; spread `MANUAL_EDIT_TABLES` after
  `RECERTIFICATION_TABLES`, and called `insertManualEdits(db, projectRoot,
  config)` in `build` right after `insertRecertifications`. The file is 238
  lines, within the 250 budget.

  two record files written directly under `openspec/manual/`.

  `manual_edits(kept_at, record, comment, paths, capabilities)` as the last
  line of `TABLE_LIST`.

  `HISTORY_TABLES` expectation, last.
