## Details

`readManualEditRecords` comes from task 2's `src/core/vcs/manual-records.ts`;
read it, don't change it. `history-hints.ts` shows how a plan prompt section is
formatted from the archive. An archived change's `archivedAt` is its `archived`
event's timestamp in `.run/events/change.jsonl`, and its `capabilities` are the
delta specs under its `specs/` folder, so the test can build one by hand.
Take the expected body from the scenario's table, not from running the code.
