## Changed

Added the document half of the specs kernel and the readers:

- `crates/kernel/specs/src/document/`: `types.rs` (the value types
  `Frontmatter`, `SyntaxError`, `Proposal`, `TaskFile`, `VerifyStarts`,
  `ChecklistItem`, `Sidecar`, `SidecarError`), `yaml.rs` (YAML field
  reading: text/list/reads/change-id fields, mapping parse with the
  file-and-line syntax error), `frontmatter.rs` (`parse_frontmatter`),
  `proposal.rs` (`parse_proposal` and the hand-written contract-table

  (`parse_task_list`), `sidecar.rs` (`parse_sidecar`) and `mod.rs`.
- `crates/kernel/specs/src/read/mod.rs`: `SpecsError` with its `Display` and
  `std::error::Error` impls, and `read_living_spec`, `read_delta`,
  `read_proposal`, `read_task_file`, `read_task_list`, `read_sidecar`.
- `crates/kernel/specs/src/lib.rs` declares and re-exports `document` and
  `read` next to `markdown`.
- `crates/kernel/specs/tests/parsing_documents.rs` and its submodules
  `parsing_documents_tasks.rs` and `parsing_documents_sidecar.rs` prove the
  frontmatter, change-id, proposal, task-file, checklist and sidecar

- `crates/kernel/specs/tests/reading.rs` proves the reading errors and one
  successful read of each kind.
- `crates/kernel/specs/tests/fixtures/documents/` holds the six small
  fixtures the successful reads use.
