## Changed

- `tests/living-specs-delta-equivalence.test.ts`: added a local
  `firstLineDifference(label, expected, actual)` helper that compares two texts
  line by line. It returns `null` when they are equal, otherwise a message with
  the caller's label, the 1-based first differing line number, and up to three
  lines of context before and after from each side. Expected lines are prefixed
  `- ` and actual lines `+ `.
- Replaced the two `assert.equal` checks in "re-seeds every living spec as the
  cumulative deterministic merge" (living spec against its replay, and the
  second replay against the first) with the helper plus `assert.fail(message)`,
  so a mismatch never prints either whole spec. Both still compare the same
  texts, including `withoutPurposeBlankLine` on the first check.
- Added a new `it` covering the proposal's "One line differs": two 500-line
  texts that differ only at line 250; the message names line 250, holds lines
  247 through 253, and holds neither line 1 nor line 500. A second case asserts
  that two equal texts give `null`.
