## Acceptance

- [ ] New `decisions/011-traceability-trial.md` follows ADR 010's layout (frontmatter, `# 011. Traceability trial`, `Date: 2026-10-06`, Status, Context, Decision, Measurement, Consequences, Rejected). Its frontmatter is `status: accepted`, `applies_to: [traceability]`, `rule: Traceability warns on osq's own traceability capability until its trial decides to block, widen, or drop it.`, and `checks: [tests/trace-own-config.test.ts]`.
- [ ] Context says, in plain prose: ADR 006 decision 4 allows a warning only while its signal is measured; traceability and mutation checks shipped with no capability opted in; change 154 opts in `traceability` alone, because it is the smallest capability (36 scenarios, 16 functions in 8 files; version-control has 98 and 58 in 20). It also says the validator (ADR 010) does not read traceability results.
- [ ] Decision says what runs: `traceability` in `warn` mode, focused tests, and StrykerJS 10 mutation checks through `tsx` with a 300-second budget per task, all observe-only. It defines a trial change as a change whose approval prints a traceability lint finding, or whose task streams hold a `focused_ran` or `mutation_ran` event. Per trial change it records four signals: link findings (each unreadable form among them), mutation picks by outcome and reason, survivors, and time added per task (the sum of that task's `focused_ran` and `mutation_ran` durations).
- [ ] Measurement says the decision is due after 8 trial changes or on 2026-12-15, whichever comes first. A human labels each link finding true (a real missing or stale link) or false, and each survivor true (a missing assertion) or false (noise), in the brief that follows. It gives these outcomes:
  - Block: `traceability` moves to `require` mode when at least three quarters of the link findings are true, at least three quarters of the picks are measured, and the median time added per task with a pick is at most 120 s.
  - Widen: `version-control` is opted in, still in `warn` mode, for one more window of the same size, when the block thresholds hold but the window holds fewer than 10 link findings and picks in all.
  - Drop: the opt-in and the mutation command are removed when fewer than half the link findings are true, fewer than half the picks are measured, or the median time added per task is over 300 s.
  - Anything else gets one more window of the same size, and then traceability must block or go.
  - Mutation checks stay only if at least one in four labeled survivors is true. Otherwise the command is dropped, whatever the link decision.
- [ ] Consequences name the cost: about 5 s for most picks, about 171 s for a pick of `scenario`, so a third pick in the same task can record `budget`. They also name what is already known: 12 prose mentions of `@scenario` in `src/` are reported unreadable, and `scanSource`'s picks are `range_unknown`. Rejected names opting in every capability at once, a homegrown mutator, and starting in `require` mode.
- [ ] `decisions/README.md`'s index gains `- [011. Traceability trial](011-traceability-trial.md)` after ADR 010's line.
