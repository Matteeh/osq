## Changed

- Added `src/core/status/wait-log.ts`: `resolveWaitLogPath` (SHA-256 of realpath under `<home>/.osq/inbox/<hash>.jsonl`, mirroring `resolveLastLookPath`), `readWaitLog` (file order, drops unreadable/unknown lines, null when absent), `waitEpisodes` (folds `seen`/`opened`/`gone` into episodes), `firstSeenTimes` (identity to `Date` for open episodes), `dispatchIdentity` (joins kind, change folder, and task number with `\u0000`, accepts `DispatchItem` and `WaitItem`), plus the `WaitRecord`, `WaitItem`, and `WaitEpisode` types.
- Added `src/core/status/wait-recorder.ts`: `createWaitRecorder` with `observe`/`opened`/`stop`, chained appends in call order, session id `<pid>-<first observe ms>`, first-observe marking of open runs and unseen items, later-observe appear/depart marking, top change detection, one stderr report per recorder on failure, and no rejecting methods. Exposes `WaitRecorder` and `WaitRecorderOptions`.
- Added `tests/wait-log.test.ts` and `tests/wait-recorder.test.ts` covering the path, fold, first-seen, unreadable-line, no-log, identity, first/later observe, same-top, stop-without-start, and unwritable-home scenarios on temporary homes with fixed dates.
- `osq inbox` wiring, dispatch order, and report consumption are left for tasks 2 through 6; no existing file outside scope was touched.

## Deviated

- The `start` record carries `idle` in addition to the listed `mode`, so the requirement "every record an observe appends SHALL carry its `at` and `idle`" holds literally. `start`'s shape is not marked "nothing more", so this is additive.
