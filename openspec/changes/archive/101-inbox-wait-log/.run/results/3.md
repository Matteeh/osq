## Changed

- Added `src/core/status/dispatch-set-aside.ts` with `applySetAsides`, moved out of `dispatch-session.ts`, now keying items by `dispatchIdentity` from `wait-log.ts` instead of a local `identityOf`.
- `src/core/status/dispatch-session.ts`: `CardSessionOptions` gains an optional `recorder: WaitRecorder`. The session resolves `home` (`options.home` defaulting to `os.homedir()`) and a `clock` (`options.now` defaulting to `new Date()`). `derive()` now reads the wait log under that home, folds it with `firstSeenTimes`, orders with those first-seen times, and calls `recorder.observe(items, dispatch.watcherIdle, at)` after every own derivation. The watch's `onItems(items, at, idle)` sets the last idle, calls `recorder.observe` (not awaited) with the set-aside-adjusted items, and keeps resolving the arrival as before. `showCard` calls `recorder.opened(first, lastIdle, clock())` when it prints a card, and `run`'s `finally` awaits `recorder.stop(clock())`. The local `identityOf` and `applySetAsides` are gone; `skip` uses `dispatchIdentity`.
- `src/core/status/dispatch-follow.ts`: `FollowDispatchOptions` gains an optional `recorder: WaitRecorder`. The preview is read with `options.home`; `recorder.observe(preview.items, preview.watcherIdle, clock())` runs at start, the watch callback observes every derivation with its `at` and `idle`, and `recorder.stop(clock())` runs after the signal aborts. The local `identityOf` is replaced by `dispatchIdentity` in `followDelta`.
- Added `tests/wait-recording.test.ts`: three scenarios on temporary project and home roots with a fixed clock, a real `createWaitRecorder`, scripted keys, a recording launcher, a fake watcher, and a manual `every` timer. They assert the exact record-type order for a card session (start, two seen, top, opened, gone, top, opened, stop), the follow loop (start, null top, seen halt with `unobserved: false`, top halt, stop), and that a session without a recorder leaves no log.

## Deviated

- The `opened` record's `at` is the clock time at which the card printed (`clock()`), while `observe` uses the derivation-start time as the requirement pins. Both come from `options.now`, so they are identical under the fixed test clock; the requirement never pinned `opened`'s `at`.
- `followDispatch` awaits the start `observe` and the final `stop` (they are cheap and ordered), while the watch's `onItems` observe stays un-awaited as the task's Details asked.
