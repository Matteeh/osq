## Changed

- Added `src/read/snapshot/markers.rs`: `TaskMarkers`, `HaltMarker` and `RunningLock`, plus the private readers for `.run/dead`, `.run/regressed`, `.run/running` and the `## Blocked` section of `.run/results`. Frontmatter goes through `osq_kernel_specs::parse_frontmatter`, the blocked section through `parse_result_sections`, and the lock through `serde_json`.
- Added `src/read/snapshot/steps.rs`: the private reader of a proposal's `## Human steps` part before approval, with case-insensitive `##`/`###` headings and the `None`/`None.` normalization.
- Gave `TaskFile` a `markers: TaskMarkers` field and `ChangeSnapshot` the `proposal`, `brief`, `before_approval` and `change_regressed` fields; `read_change` and `read_task_files` now fill them.
- Re-exported `TaskMarkers`, `HaltMarker` and `RunningLock` from `read/mod.rs` and `lib.rs`, and named the new requirements in the readers' docs.
- Added `tests/reading_markers.rs` for the halted and unreadable marker scenarios and `tests/reading_planning.rs` for the planning facts scenario, each on folders written under `CARGO_TARGET_TMPDIR`.

Touched: crates/kernel/storage/src/lib.rs, crates/kernel/storage/src/read/mod.rs, crates/kernel/storage/src/read/snapshot.rs, crates/kernel/storage/src/read/snapshot/folder.rs, crates/kernel/storage/src/read/snapshot/markers.rs, crates/kernel/storage/src/read/snapshot/steps.rs, crates/kernel/storage/tests/reading_markers.rs, crates/kernel/storage/tests/reading_planning.rs
