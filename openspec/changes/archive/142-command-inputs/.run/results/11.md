## Changed

- `src/cli/lint.ts`: `LintCommandOptions` now extends `CommandInputs` (dropping its own `cwd`, `config` and `stdout`), keeps `logger`, and resolves inputs through `resolveInputs`. Without a `logger` it logs through `commandLogger(options)`; JSON writes its document plus `\n` to the resolved `stdout`.
- `src/cli/migrate.ts`: `MigrateCommandOptions` now extends `CommandInputs` (dropping its own `cwd` and `config`), keeps `logger`, and resolves `cwd`/`config` through `resolveInputs`. Without a `logger` it logs through `commandLogger(options)`.
- `src/cli/watch.ts`: `watchCommand` takes `WatchCommandOptions & CommandInputs`, uses the resolved `cwd`/`config`, and builds its logger with `commandLogger(options, resolveLogLevel(options))`. The `--dev` supervisor path is unchanged.
- `tests/command-inputs-logger.test.ts`: new tests comparing `osq lint <id>`, `osq lint --json <id>` and `osq migrate legacy` directly against `runCliCaptured`, plus a `watchCommand` call on an empty project asserting nothing reaches the process streams.

## Outside scope

- `tests/report-reads-once.test.ts` failed once during a full `pnpm verify` with a transient `ENOENT .../fixture/report/.osq/index.sqlite-shm` while copying the fixture; it passes in isolation and the final full `pnpm verify` was green. Left alone as a pre-existing flake unrelated to these commands.
