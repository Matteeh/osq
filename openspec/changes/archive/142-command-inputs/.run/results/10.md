## Changed

- `src/cli/plan.ts`: `PlanCommandOptions` now extends `CommandInputs` and no
  longer declares its own `cwd`. `planCommand` builds `resolveInputs(options)`
  and takes `cwd`, `input.config()` (at the same point `loadConfig(cwd)` sat)
  and the `stdout`/`stderr` writers from it. `--print` writes
  `` `${prompt}\n` `` through `inputs.stdout`; the non-session handoff passes
  `inputs.stdout` to a new local `writePromptHandoff`. The `quiet` boolean is
  replaced by `createdOutput: Writer | null`, set to `inputs.stdout` only for
  `--session`. `prepareQueueSelection` receives `inputs.stderr`.
- `src/cli/plan-queue.ts`: removed `writePromptHandoff` (moved to its only
  caller). `prepareQueueSelection` takes an optional `stderr: Writer`
  defaulting to `processStderr` and writes the notice plus `\n` to it.
  `createChange` and `createQueueChange` take `output: Writer | null` in place
  of `quiet` and write the two created lines, each plus `\n`, through it.
- `tests/command-inputs-plan.test.ts` (new): runs `osq plan --next --print`
  through `runCliCaptured` and directly through `planCommand` with appending
  `cwd`, `config`, `stdout` and `stderr` on two resets of the same project
  path, asserting identical stdout and stderr and no process-stream leak; a
  second case asserts the handoff line reaches the passed `stdout`.
- `tests/plan-command-error.test.ts`: the incomplete-coverage notice is read
  from a passed `stderr` writer instead of a patched `console.error`, asserting
  the same text and its trailing newline.
