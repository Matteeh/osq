## Details

A chime of two short sine tones, such as 880 Hz then 1320 Hz, at 8000
samples per second, with a fade in and out on each tone so it does not
click, fits in the size limit. Round each sample with `Math.round` so the
bytes are the same on every run. `tests/package-hygiene.test.ts` is the only
existing test this change breaks; its `isAllowedPath` and the
`packs only distribution assets` test are the places to extend.
