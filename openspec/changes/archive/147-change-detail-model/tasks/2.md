- [ ] `showCommand` gets the details from `getSpecDetails` and prints `formatShowJson` or `formatShowText`; it no longer imports `src/core/spec/digest.ts`, and its return value, printed text and `Show error:` wrapping are unchanged.

`openspec/changes/147-change-detail-model/`.
