## Details

Bound the export by `timeouts.harnessPreflightSeconds`, 10 when unset, as
`checkHarness` in `src/core/foundation/doctor.ts` does. Track the streamed
tokens and cost for the run in the parser's closure, and compute the
remainder field by field: prompt tokens from `input`, candidate tokens from
`output`, reasoning from `reasoning`, cached from `cache.read` plus
`cache.write`, total as prompt plus candidate. Append the remainder only when
some field is above zero, and never a negative field. The fake binary in the
test answers `run ...` by printing the fixture stream and
`session export ...` by printing the fixture export. Keep every new function
within 80 lines; `buildOpencodeArgs` is grandfathered and must stay above 80.
