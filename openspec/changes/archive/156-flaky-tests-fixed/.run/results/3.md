## Changed

- `src/watcher/loop.ts`: `startWatcher` now checks `stopped`/`interrupted`
  again after `await changeTrees(...)` and returns before creating the chokidar
  watcher or the poll interval. A `stoppedPromise` settles only when the watcher
  is stopped and no cycle is in flight; `stop()` settles it when idle, and
  `cycleHandler`'s `finally` settles it once a running cycle ends. With a
  signal, the returned promise now awaits that settlement; without one it
  resolves after setup as before.
- `tests/watch-service-build.test.ts`: the two tests that started the watcher
  with `void` now keep the promise and `await` it after `controller.abort()`
  instead of `await delay(50)`; `afterEach` drops `maxRetries`/`retryDelay`.
- `tests/watcher-abort.test.ts` (new): proves abort during a cycle resolves
  only after the aborting `buildCheck` returns and starts no further cycle, and
  sweeps abort after 0–30 `setImmediate` turns for abort during setup.
