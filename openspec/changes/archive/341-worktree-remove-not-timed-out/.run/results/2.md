## Changed

- `restartDefaultBranchBranch` wraps `worktreeRemove` and throws
  `could not remove worktree <path>: <git's message>` plus the recovery line,
  before renaming the branch or touching any marker.
- `approveDefaultBranchSteering` reads `vcs.status()` and skips the
  `osq: <id> replanned` commit when no entry sits under the change folder, so a
  second approval after a failed restart no longer fails with `nothing to
  commit`.
- Added `tests/recovery-restart-remove-fails.test.ts`, proving "Worktree
  removal fails during a restart": the locked worktree stops the first
  approval with the recovery text, leaves `.run/regressed/change.md` byte for
  byte and the branches untouched, and after unlocking, a second approval
  restarts the change.
