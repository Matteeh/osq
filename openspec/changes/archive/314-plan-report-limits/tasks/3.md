`include!` and calls its private `fit_lines` and `newest_first`, so keep
those two names and signatures, and keep the file free of `super::` paths
other than `leading_number`. Convert the config's `u64` sizes to `usize`
in `report.rs`. Run the proposal's verify with
`OSQ_CHANGE=openspec/changes/314-plan-report-limits`.
