## Acceptance

- [ ] `cutStackedChange` no longer removes the worktree when the cut fails, and `removeFailedWorktree` is gone. A failed cut rethrows its error, and the branch, the worktree, and the stacked approval stay, as the watcher-and-harness delta's "Stacked cut" says.
- [ ] New `tests/stack-cut-kept.test.ts` covers the "Cut fails and resumes" scenario through `runWatcherOnce` and `retrySpec`. After the failed cycle, the worktree directory exists at `worktreePath` and `git worktree list` names it on `osq/<folder>`. The cycle after the retry runs in that same path and ends with exactly one `osq: <id> approved` commit and no stacked approval.

## Details

Build the project as `tests/stack-run.test.ts` builds it for "halts on a
failed prepare and resumes after retry". Use its `makeProject`,
`ActingAdapter`, and a `prepare` script that fails while a marker file
exists, and copy those helpers into the new file. The retry depends on task
1's resolver: a kept worktree that does not hold the change no longer hides
the stacked tree.
