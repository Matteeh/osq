## Acceptance

- [ ] `changeTrees` gives an osq worktree a tree only when the worktree holds its change, as the status-inspection delta's "Change locations" says. A worktree that does not hold it hides neither the checkout's copy nor a stacked tree of that folder.
- [ ] `change-locations.ts` exports `matchesFolder`. `stack-dependencies.ts` imports it and drops its own copy.
- [ ] In `tests/change-locations-stacked.test.ts` ("prefers a worktree over a stacked directory for the same folder") and `tests/serve-sse-worktrees.test.ts` ("passes the resolved worktree trees to the invalidation hub"), the worktree's copy of the change gets a `.run/approved` file before the assertions. Nothing else in those two files changes.
- [ ] New `tests/change-locations-holds.test.ts` covers the delta's scenarios "Worktree without its change", "Worktree with an unapproved copy", and "Archived or rejected in its worktree" through `changeTrees`, `listChanges`, and `findChange`.

## Details

Build repositories as `tests/change-locations-stacked.test.ts` does, with
real `git worktree add` on `osq/` branches, and write stacked directories as
its `writeStacked` does. Check each of the three paths with a single
`fs.stat` each, in the order the delta lists them, and stop at the first
that exists.
