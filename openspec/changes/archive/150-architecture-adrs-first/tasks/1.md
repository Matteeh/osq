## Details

The starter's `## ` questions are short and literal, one or two sentences
each, so a planner can answer them in place. Keep the frontmatter to `status`
and `applies_to` only: an accepted ADR without a `rule` fails validation,
which is what should happen if someone accepts the starter unanswered.
