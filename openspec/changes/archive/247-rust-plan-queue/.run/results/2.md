## Changed

- `crates/features/plan/Cargo.toml` adds `osq-kernel-storage.workspace = true` and `sha2.workspace = true`; `Cargo.lock` records them on `osq-feature-plan`.
- `src/queue/parse.rs` gives every item its `sha256:` hash over its raw text with `sha2::Sha256`, in lowercase hex.
- New `src/queue/links.rs` groups change folders by item and location, builds the archived-first ambiguity messages, and chooses each item's state, change id, rejection count and changed-since-planned flag.
- `src/queue/text.rs` builds each row from the chosen state and leaves landed slugs out of the unmet list.
- `src/queue.rs` reads change folders with `osq_kernel_storage::list_changes`, reads each `brief.md` through `frontmatter_string`, checks an active folder's `proposal.md` or `spec.md`, and reads the one active folder's status through `read_change`.
- New `tests/queue_states.rs` proves "Changed since planned", "Ambiguous associations" and every row of "States of single items" on repositories under `CARGO_TARGET_TMPDIR`.
- New `tests/queue_golden.rs`, `tests/fixtures/golden/` and `tests/fixtures/queue.txt` prove "Golden queue output" on a fresh scratch copy, byte for byte.

## Deviated

- A `read_change` refusal for an active folder counts as no active folder and falls through to rejected or unplanned, as rust-plan "Queue states" and its `not json` row require, rather than failing `queue` with `FeatureError::failed`.
- The `queue_item` empty-string check was folded into the slug-membership check, since an empty value names no item and the separate check left an equivalent `cargo-mutants` mutant.

## Missing context

- The task's "Verify Command" is `cargo test -p osq-feature-plan ...`, but `sh scripts/verify-rust.sh` needs `OSQ_CHANGE=openspec/changes/247-rust-plan-queue` for the link-gate corpus test to see the delta requirements.
