## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sums lists
The system SHALL sum a list.

#### Scenario: Sum of three
- **WHEN** one, two and three are summed
- **THEN** the sum is six

### Requirement: Adds numbers
The system SHALL add two whole numbers.

#### Scenario: Two plus two
- **WHEN** two and two are added
- **THEN** the sum is four
