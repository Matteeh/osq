## Details

ADR 017 is `decisions/017-language-support.md`, which 185 adds; this change stacks on 185, so it is in your tree. Kernel slice under ADR 016: until the `git` and `config` kernel slices move, `language-map.ts` imports `selectVcs` from `src/core/vcs/select.ts` and the `OsqConfig` type from `src/core/foundation/config.ts`; it imports nothing else outside its folder. Until archive, `tests/capability-slices.test.ts` prints the two files as unowned; that is a diagnostic, not a failure.
