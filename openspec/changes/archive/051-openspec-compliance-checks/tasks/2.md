## Acceptance

- [ ] New `src/core/spec/openspec-version.ts` reads the `@fission-ai/openspec` range from `peerDependencies` in osq's own `package.json` via `PACKAGE_ROOT`. It classifies a version as `pinned` (equal to `OPENSPEC_EXPECTED_VERSION`), `in-range`, or `out-of-range`. An unreadable range, an unparseable version, or a prerelease version is `out-of-range`.
- [ ] `validateWithOpenSpec` uses the classification. `pinned` passes as today. `in-range` adds a warning naming the version, the range, and ADR 005. `out-of-range` keeps today's error text unchanged.
- [ ] Doctor's `validator` check does the same. `pinned` keeps `pinned 1.13.1`. `in-range` passes with a warning flag and the message `openspec version <v> differs from pinned <pin>; inside supported range <range> (ADR 005)`. `out-of-range` keeps today's failing message unchanged.
- [ ] `DoctorCheckResult` gains an optional warning flag. `doctorCommand` prints `[warn] <name>: <message>` for such a check, and the check doesn't count as a failure.
- [ ] New `tests/openspec-version.test.ts` covers the range grammar and each classification. It also runs `runDoctorChecks` with an injected `probeValidator` of an in-range version, and `doctorCommand` output with its exit code, and the linter with an in-range fake validator.
- [ ] `decisions/005-openspec-validator-peer-range.md` records the policy in the Details and is indexed in `decisions/README.md`. `decisions/004-pinned-openspec-validator.md` is unchanged.
- [ ] The task's verify command passes, with `tests/doctor.test.ts`, `tests/linter.test.ts`, and `tests/validator-missing.test.ts` unchanged.

## Details

This task's `scope` is authoritative over any injected code-ownership rules.

The existing drift tests use `1.12.0` and `9.9.9`, both outside `>=1.13.1 <2`,
and they pin today's messages. Keep those messages byte for byte. Neither the
pin nor the range may appear as a literal in code: the pin stays
`OPENSPEC_EXPECTED_VERSION`, and the range comes from `package.json`. Support
the comparators `>=`, `>`, `<=`, `<`, and `=` on `major.minor.patch`, separated
by spaces, with every comparator required to hold. Do not add a dependency.

`validateWithOpenSpec` is 68 lines, and the function budget caps functions at 80.
Keep the new branching in `openspec-version.ts`, and have `validateWithOpenSpec`
call one helper that returns the error or the warning. The word `skipped` must
not appear anywhere in `src/`.

For `tests/openspec-version.test.ts`, the fake-validator helpers in
`tests/linter.test.ts` and `tests/validator-missing.test.ts` show how to install
a fake `node_modules/@fission-ai/openspec/package.json` and `.bin/openspec`.
Write your own copies in the new file.

### ADR 005 content

- Title `# 005. OpenSpec Validator Peer Range`, a `## Status` of `Accepted`, and
  sections `Context`, `Decision`, and `Consequences`, like ADR 004.
- It supersedes ADR 004's drift rule for doctor, lint, and approve. ADR 004's
  exact `devDependencies` pin and its execution flags still stand.
- The policy: the pin passes; a different version inside the
  `peerDependencies` range warns; outside the range fails. The warning texts
  above appear verbatim in the ADR.
- The scheduled `OpenSpec latest` workflow runs the differential test against
  `@latest`. A failure means osq and upstream diverge, and moving the pin or the
  range needs its own ADR.
- Archive folders keep osq's `<id>-<slug>` naming, and are not renamed to
  OpenSpec's `<date>-<id>-<slug>`. The differential test compares living specs
  only.
