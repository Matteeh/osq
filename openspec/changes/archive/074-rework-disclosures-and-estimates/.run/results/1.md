## Changed

- `SpecData` gains `fixes`; `parseSpecMd` reads frontmatter `fixes` through a shared `normalizeChangeIds` helper matching `depends_on` normalization, with absent/non-array reading as `[]`.
- `lintChangeFolder` reports `fixes names missing change: <id>` for every `fixes` id `checkDependencyExists` cannot find, beside the `depends_on` check.
- `QueueItem` gains `fixes`; `parseSection` consumes an optional `Fixes:` line as the next non-empty line after `Depends on:`, rejecting empty values, invalid/self/forward/unknown/repeated slugs, and a second `Fixes:` line with queue path and item context. The line is excluded from the body and the raw-section hash is unchanged.
- `queue-state.ts` folds an item's unlanded `fixes` into `unmetDependencies` after its unlanded dependencies.
- `QueuePlanSelection` gains `landedFixes`, resolved through a shared `resolveLanded` helper; `createQueueChange` passes their ids as `fixes` through `createChange` to `createNewSpec`, and `seedProposal` inserts `fixes: [...]` right after `depends_on` only when non-empty.
- Added `tests/fixes-declaration.test.ts` covering parsing, the lint error, all invalid `Fixes:` cases, the waiting rule, and `osq plan --next --print` seeding a landed fix.
