## Changed

- `src/core/vcs/sync-stop.ts`: new `SyncStop` `Error` subclass with readonly
  `reason` of `sync_conflict` or `sync_failed`.
- `src/core/vcs/sync-specs.ts`: `assertRequirementsUnchanged` now throws a
  `sync_failed` `SyncStop`; `rebuildLivingSpecs` takes an `archived` flag and
  the merge's conflicts, resolves conflicting archive paths from the default
  branch, rewrites every capability folder under the merged worktree's living
  specs from the default branch, applies deltas only when archived, and stages
  every file it writes or removes.
- `src/core/vcs/sync-verify.ts`: new step 5. Reads done, non-manual tasks and
  their `verify` through `deriveSpecState`/`readChangeFolder`, reads a done
  marker's `manual` flag with `parseFrontmatter`, imports nothing from
  `src/watcher`, runs an archived change's proposal verify or an active
  change's task verifies through `runVerificationCommand`, stops with a
  `sync_failed` `SyncStop` on a failure, appends `verify_ran` and `synced`
  events with `fs` directly.
- `src/core/vcs/sync-main.ts`: keeps its signature; handles active and
  archived changes, snapshots and restores `.run/events/change.jsonl` on every
  abort, throws `SyncStop` from every stop, keeps the code-conflict stop as
  `sync_conflict` and wraps every other post-merge failure as `sync_failed`,
  and builds the active/archived verify suffix.
- `tests/vcs-sync-active.test.ts`: new tests covering "Active change before
  its first task", "Active change re-runs done tasks", "Red task verify after
  the merge", "Code conflict in an active change", "Failed commit keeps
  uncommitted events", and "Stacked dependent after its dependency landed" on
  real temporary repositories.
