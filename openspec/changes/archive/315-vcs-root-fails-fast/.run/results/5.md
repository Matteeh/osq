## Changed

- `crates/features/land/src/land.rs`: `run_land` now calls rust-vcs
  `project_git_root` right after `load_config`, maps a `NestedRoot` to `land
  failed: <display>`, keeps its "osq land needs vcs.enabled and git" text when
  the kernel gives no folder, and uses the kernel's folder where it read
  `vcs.root()`. Its doc gained `Implements: rust-vcs/project-root-check`.
- `crates/features/land/src/land/sync_command.rs`: `sync` calls
  `project_git_root` right after loading the config, maps a `NestedRoot` to
  `sync failed: <display>`, and keeps "osq sync needs vcs.enabled and git"
  when there is no folder; its own `vcs.enabled` and `root()` checks are gone.
- `crates/features/land/src/land/message_command.rs`: `message` does the same,
  mapping a `NestedRoot` to `message failed: <display>` and keeping "osq
  message needs vcs.enabled and git".
- Added `crates/features/land/tests/land_nested_root.rs`, which builds a fresh
  `<outer>` repository under `CARGO_TARGET_TMPDIR` with the nested fixture,
  runs the `land`, `sync` and `message` rows through `LandFeature::run` on
  `<outer>/sub`, and checks the refusal message and that `<outer>`'s status,
  branches, worktrees and files outside `.git` are unchanged.
- Ran `cargo fmt --all`, the task verify, every `osq-feature-land` test, and the
  proposal verify with `OSQ_CHANGE=openspec/changes/315-vcs-root-fails-fast`;
  all pass. A cargo-mutants run over the changed source lines found 6 mutants,
  all caught.
