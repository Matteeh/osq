## Changed

- `packages/ui/src/contracts.ts`: re-exports, as types only, `ApprovalNotices`,
  `ApprovalNotice` and `NoticeSeverity` from `src/core/spec/notices.js`.
- New `packages/ui/src/change/NoticePanel.tsx`: renders the "Notice block
  view" — a `Notices` `h3`, `Nothing unusual` when `unusual` is false, one
  `<details>` per shown notice with a `<summary>` naming the severity word
  (`Red`/`Amber`/`Grey`) and the label and the detail below it, and one
  `<details>` summarised `<k> more` holding the rest. Each notice's
  `onToggle` calls the `onOpen(id)` prop when it opens.
- New `packages/ui/src/change/notice-state.ts`: exports `unopenedRedLabels`
  (the labels of red notices whose ids are not in `opened`, in notice order)
  and `approveInput` (`{ verb: 'approve', opened }` with the ids sorted, or
  `{ verb: 'approve' }` when `opened` is undefined). `actions-client.ts`'s
  `WebActionInput` approve member gained `readonly opened?: readonly string[]`.
- `ChangeView.tsx` keeps the opened ids in React state; for a review with
  `notices` it renders `NoticePanel` after the header and error and before
  `BriefPanel`, and passes `ChangeActionsPanel` the opened ids and
  `unopenedRedLabels`. `ChangeActionsPanel` posts `approveInput(opened)` for
  an approve tap, with `opened` undefined when the review has no notices.
- `ChangeActions.tsx` takes optional `approveBlockedBy` labels; while it is
  non-empty the Approve button is disabled and its item shows
  `Open each red notice to approve: <label>, <label>`.
- New `packages/ui/src/change/notices.css`, imported by `main.tsx` next to
  `review.css`: `notice-red`, `notice-amber` and `notice-grey` classes draw a
  `border-inline-start` stripe in `var(--status-dead)`,
  `var(--status-regressed)` or `var(--status-pending)`, and the notice text
  wraps with `overflow-wrap: anywhere`.
- `change/index.ts` exports `NoticePanel` and the two notice-state helpers.
- New `tests/ui-notices.test.tsx` covers every "Notice block view" scenario
  with `renderToStaticMarkup`: the section leads the brief, `Nothing unusual`
  with a grey-only review, five shown notices and one `2 more` folded item,
  `ChangeActions` disabled with `approveBlockedBy` and enabled without it,
  `unopenedRedLabels` and `approveInput` for the opened case, `notices.css`
  stripe tokens and `overflow-wrap` for phone width, and a document built by
  `getWebChange` for a change that removes a requirement shows the
  `removes requirements` notice.

The task verify (`TSX_TSCONFIG_PATH=packages/ui/tsconfig.runtime.json node
--import tsx --import ./tests/git-test-env.ts --test tests/ui-notices.test.tsx`)
passes (6 tests), and the proposal verify `pnpm verify` passes: both
typechecks, the build, every CLI and UI test, and lint.
