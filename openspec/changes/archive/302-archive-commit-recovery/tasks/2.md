## Acceptance

- [ ] An archived change in an osq worktree whose `.run/regressed/change.md` has reason `commit_failed` gets the next step, inbox item and dispatch item status-inspection "Archived change with a failed archive commit" says, with the detail "Next step detail and format" names.
- [ ] New `tests/status-archive-commit.test.ts` proves "Failed archive commit in show and the inbox" and "Other archived halts unchanged" with `scenario()` from `@matteeh/osq/testing`, building the archived worktree change as `tests/steering-archived.test.ts` does and writing the marker by hand.
- [ ] Every existing test passes unchanged, and `pnpm verify` passes.

## Details

Add a lister beside `listArchivedSteering` in `steering-change.ts` for
archived worktree changes whose marker reason is `commit_failed` and
whose derived state has no steering, and use it from
`inbox-projection.ts` and `dispatch-items.ts`. `inbox-projection.ts` and
`dispatch-items.ts` are near the 250-line budget; keep the item builders
small or move one into `steering-change.ts`. `readNotLanded` in
`status.ts` already drops a change whose next step is not `archived`.

## Scenarios

- status-inspection: Failed archive commit in show and the inbox
- status-inspection: Other archived halts unchanged
