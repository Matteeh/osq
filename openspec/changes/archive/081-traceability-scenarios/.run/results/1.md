## Changed

- `src/core/spec/delta.ts`: exported `ScenarioOutcome` (`text` plus optional
  `rows` of `Record<string, string>`), added `outcomes` to `DeltaScenario`, and
  rewrote `parseScenario` to collect every THEN line and every AND line that
  follows a THEN with no WHEN between, pairing each outcome with the Markdown
  table directly below it. New helpers `outcomeWithTable`, `splitTableRow`,
  `isTableSeparator`, and `readTableRows` handle indented tables, blank lines
  under the outcome, header-keyed rows, and empty missing cells. `when` and
  `then` keep their previous values.
- `tests/scenario-outcomes.test.ts`: new suite covering both "Scenario
  outcomes" scenarios plus an indented table, blank lines under an outcome, a
  missing cell, and a table under an AND line. It lints a temporary scaffolded
  project whose delta adds the pricing sample's "Volume pricing" requirement
  with its table and asserts the change is valid with no finding mentioning a
  table, and runs the local `node_modules/.bin/openspec validate pricing --type
  spec --strict --json --no-interactive` under `OPENSPEC_TELEMETRY=0` against a
  temporary copy of the sample living spec, asserting it is valid with no
  issues.
