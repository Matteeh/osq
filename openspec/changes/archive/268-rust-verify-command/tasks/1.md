## Acceptance

- [ ] New `crates/kernel/harnesses/src/verify.rs`, with any submodules under `src/verify/`, holds `VerifyRequest`, `VerifyOutcome` and `run_verify`, exported from `lib.rs`, as rust-harnesses "Verify command", "Verify environment" and "Verify outcome" say. `VerifyRequest` has the fields `command`, `cwd`, `source_env`, `configured_env`, `agent_env`, `change`, `diff`, `extra_env`, `timeout` and `kill_grace`.
- [ ] The environment comes from the runners kernel's `role_env` with `EnvRole::Verify`; the process group comes from `std::os::unix::process::CommandExt::process_group(0)`, and signals reach the group the way `process/runner.rs` sends them, through the system `kill` with a negative process id. Reuse `ProcessError` and anything in `src/process/` you can call as it is; do not change files under `src/process/`.
- [ ] The relative-path rewrite and the outcome mapping are pure functions, and only the orchestrator starts the process (ADR 023).
- [ ] New `crates/kernel/harnesses/tests/verify_command.rs` proves every scenario below, one test per scenario, each doc naming `Covers: rust-harnesses/<scenario>`.

## Details

Never read anything under `src/` from Rust; the TypeScript files are in
