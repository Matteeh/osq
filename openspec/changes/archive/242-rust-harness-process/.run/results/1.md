## Changed

- Added `crates/kernel/harnesses/src/process.rs` with `ProcessSpec`,
  `ProcessOutcome`, `ProcessError`, and the re-export of `run_process`; the
  error type implements `Display` and `std::error::Error`.
- Added `src/process/runner.rs` with spawn, the channel receive loop and the
  system `kill` calls, `src/process/readers.rs` with the stdout, stderr and
  wait threads, and `src/process/state.rs` with the deadline and exit-code
  state machine.
- The child is spawned after `env_clear()` and the declared environment, with
  stdin closed and both output pipes read by helper threads. stdout lines go
  to the calling thread on one channel in arrival order; blank lines are
  dropped, a trailing partial line is passed, and bytes are lossily decoded.
- Added `tests/process_runner.rs` with every table row of "Runs of small
  scripts", "Stderr, folder and stop timing", and "Program that cannot start".
- Declared `mod process;` and re-exported the four public items in `src/lib.rs`.

## Deviated

- The tests add two cases beyond the scenario tables so the grace rules are
  observable rather than timing-dependent: `stop_grace_precedes_kill` checks a
  TERM trap that exits after a short sleep and before `kill_grace`; and
  `stdout_stays_open_for_the_grace_after_exit` checks that a late line from a
  background child is still delivered during the stdout grace.
- When the stdout grace expires after the program exited, a stderr reader that
  has not reported yet is treated as empty. Without that, the table row
  `(sleep 10) & echo done` would wait for the background child to close stderr
  and take 10 seconds, since it inherits both pipes. Normal runs report stderr
  well before the grace expires.
