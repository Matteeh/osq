## Acceptance

- [ ] `rust-toolchain.toml` holds a `[toolchain]` table with `channel = "1.99.1"` and `components = ["clippy", "rustfmt"]`, and nothing else.
- [ ] In `.github/workflows/rust.yml`, each of the `cargo`, `mutants` and `parity` jobs replaces its `dtolnay/rust-toolchain@stable` step with a step named `Install the pinned toolchain` whose `run` is exactly `rustup toolchain install`, in the same position: after checkout (and, in `parity`, after the pnpm and Node setup), before `Swatinem/rust-cache@`. No other step, trigger or job changes.
- [ ] `tests/rust-ci.test.ts` gains a `describe('rust-core: Rust toolchain is pinned once', ...)` with one case per table row of that scenario. It reads `rust-toolchain.toml` with plain text matching; add no TOML dependency.
- [ ] `tests/rust-ci.test.ts`'s `TOOLCHAIN` constant and its uses move to the new step: the mutation-check case compares the toolchain step of `cargo` and `mutants` by `run` instead of `uses`, and the parity case's first row checks for the `rustup toolchain install` step in place of `dtolnay/rust-toolchain@`, as rust-core "Rust CI runs the parity tests" now says. No other existing case changes.
- [ ] `pnpm lint` reports nothing in `tests/rust-ci.test.ts`.

## Details

Reuse `stepsOf`, `stepRunning` and `stepUsing` in `tests/rust-ci.test.ts`.
For the ordering row, find the index of the install step and check that
it is lower than the index of the cache step and of every step whose
`run` holds `cargo` or `sh scripts/`. `Cargo.toml`'s `rust-version` stays
`1.85`. It is the minimum supported version, not the pinned one.
