## Acceptance

- [ ] `DispatchKind` loses `verify`. `verifyItems`, `VerifyCard`, `verifyCard`, and `verifyLines` go. `readDispatchItems` no longer reads the overview's pending verifications. `KIND_RANK` keeps the three kinds. This covers the status-inspection delta's "Dispatch items" and "Dispatch order".
- [ ] `LandCard` gains `check`, from `readCheckCommand` on the proposal's frontmatter, and `afterLanding`, from `parseHumanSteps` on its body. The land lines print them after the squash message with the existing `scalar` and `block` helpers, as `check` and `after landing`, so a null or empty value prints nothing. This covers "Dispatch cards".
- [ ] `cardKeys` drops the `check` and `verified` verbs and their cases, so those commands come back as manual. This covers "Card keys".
- [ ] `NeedsYouKind` loses `verification-pending` and `verification-failed`. `projectPendingVerifications` leaves `inbox-projection.ts`, the two text lines leave `inbox-text.ts`, and the two labels leave `packages/ui/src/home/labels.ts`. This covers "Stable inbox object" and the web-inspection delta's "Inbox kind labels".
- [ ] `report-inbox-wait.ts` keeps `verify` in its kinds through a local type, `DispatchKind | 'verify'`, for wait logs written before this change. The file grows by at most two lines and stays at or under 250. This covers the metrics-and-reporting delta's "Report without verification counts" for the `Inbox waiting` section. `src/core/status/wait-log.ts` does not change.
- [ ] Each listed test that expects a verify item, a verification kind or label, or the `c`, `p`, or `f` key now expects what the deltas say. `tests/dispatch-order.test.ts`'s idle-watcher case uses a heavy `land` item.
- [ ] New `tests/dispatch-no-verify.test.ts` covers "Each kind", "Verify card", "Verify keys", and "Archived change with after-landing steps" from the status-inspection delta.
