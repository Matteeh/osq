## Acceptance

- [ ] `runAutomaticRetries` retries a `provider_unavailable` death through `retrySpec` with `automatic: true` only after `gates.providerRetryDelaySeconds` have passed since the task's latest `dead` event; before that it does nothing and decides again on a later cycle.
- [ ] Provider retries are capped by `gates.providerRetries`, counted from automatic `retry` events whose `reason` is `provider_unavailable` since the later of the manifest's `approvedAt` and the last manual retry. They never count toward `gates.autoRetries`, and `gates.autoRetries: 0` still turns every automatic retry off.
- [ ] A `provider_unavailable` death is never marked stuck: no `stuck: true`, no `stuck` event, no stuck line, even when its fingerprint equals the previous death's. Every other reason keeps today's stuck and retry behaviour.
- [ ] New `src/watcher/provider-retry.ts` holds the provider decision, so `auto-retry.ts` stays under 250 lines and `decideDeadTask` under 80.
- [ ] README's `## Gates and permissions` names `provider_unavailable`, its wait, its own budget and that it is never stuck, in the Automatic retry and Stuck tasks bullets; its dead reasons list names `provider_unavailable`; and the three `gates.provider*` keys appear with their defaults and what they do, including the early stop.
- [ ] `tests/provider-retry.test.ts` covers each line above through `runAutomaticRetries`, with dead markers and event streams written to a temporary change folder, including two `provider_unavailable` deaths with the same fingerprint under `gates.autoRetries: 1`, and a `verify_red` death after them that still gets its automatic retry.

## Details

cross the delay without sleeping.
