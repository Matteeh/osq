## Acceptance

- [ ] New `src/watcher/fingerprint.ts` exports `stripAnsi(text)`, `normalizeFailureBody(body, projectRoot?)`, and `markerFingerprint(content, projectRoot?)`. The fingerprint is `sha256:<hex>` of the frontmatter `reason`, a newline, and the normalized body after the frontmatter.
- [ ] Normalization strips ANSI escape sequences and replaces ISO-8601 timestamps with `<time>`, durations such as `12ms`, `3.4s`, `(812.5ms)`, or `2m` with `<duration>`, `pid: 123`, `pid 123`, or `PID=123` with `pid <pid>`, and any absolute path under `projectRoot` with `<root>`.
- [ ] `writeDeadMarker(runDir, taskNumber, content, projectRoot?)` inserts `fingerprint: sha256:<hex>` as a frontmatter line. Content without frontmatter gains a frontmatter block holding only the fingerprint. Every other byte of the marker is unchanged.
- [ ] Every dead marker written by `runTask` and `spawnTaskAgent` passes `projectRoot`. The stale-lock reaper in `loop.ts` is left to task 4.
- [ ] New `tests/dead-fingerprint.test.ts` shows that markers with the same reason differing only in timestamps, durations, PIDs, ANSI codes, or the project root share a fingerprint. Markers differing in reason or in a failing test name do not. A marker written through a real `runTask` death carries the field.
- [ ] `outcome.ts`, `runner.ts`, and `spawn.ts` stay under 200 lines (`tests/import-graph.test.ts`, `tests/runner-outcome-logging.test.ts`).

## Details

A worktree measurement showed that putting the normalization inside
`outcome.ts` pushes it to 200 lines and fails both lifecycle budget tests. Keep
`outcome.ts` to the extra parameter and one call. `runner.ts` is at 197 lines;
pass the project root through its existing `fail` helper without adding lines.
Other tests are frozen.
