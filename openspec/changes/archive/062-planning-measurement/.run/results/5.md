## Changed

- Rewrote `src/harness/codex/codex-observe-usage.ts` around a small
  `CodexState` accumulator:
  - `turns`: one `PlanningTurn` per `event_msg` `token_count` carrying
    `info.last_token_usage`, timestamped at the record. A `token_count` whose
    normalized `info.total_token_usage` equals the previous `token_count`'s adds
    no turn, and `token_usage_record` lines add none.
  - Turn usage reads `last_token_usage`: `inputTokens` is
    `input_tokens - cached_input_tokens` clamped at 0, `cacheReadTokens` is
    `cached_input_tokens`, `cacheWriteTokens` is `cache_write_input_tokens`,
    `outputTokens` is `output_tokens`, `reasoningTokens` is
    `reasoning_output_tokens`, and `cost` is null. Each field is independently
    null when missing or non-numeric. The turn model is the latest
    `turn_context` or `session_meta` model.
  - Edits buffer and attach to the next turn: still successful `apply_patch`
    custom/function calls, plus `event_msg` `patch_apply_end` with
    `success: true` whose `changes` keys are the paths. `changes` bodies are
    never read. Edits left after the last turn flush into one final turn with
    null usage at the last edit's timestamp.
  - The session carries `harnessVersion` from `session_meta.cli_version` and
    `sessionCost: null`, and no longer sets `startedAt`/`endedAt`.
- `tests/planning-observed-codex.test.ts`: updated only the session-level
  cumulative usage, start/end, and `edits`-from-`turns` assertions. Test 1 now
  expects one final edit turn with null usage, null `harnessVersion`, null
  `sessionCost`, and `undefined` start/end; test 2 now expects no turn, null
  usage, and no edits for a `token_count` without `last_token_usage`.
- Added `tests/planning-codex-turns.test.ts` and the content-free fixture
  `tests/fixtures/planning-codex/` (`source.json` with
  `{"harness":"codex","version":"0.154.0-alpha.6.2"}` and `rollout.jsonl` with
  `__CWD__`/`__INSIDE_PATH__` placeholders). The test renders the fixture into a
  temporary Codex data home and drives `readCodexPlanningSessions`: it asserts
  two turns, the cache-adjusted input (`16664-11136`, `3336-864`), the
  `patch_apply_end` edit on the second turn, that the duplicate
  `token_usage_record` and repeated `token_count` add no turn, and that the
  `exec` custom tool call adds no edit.

## Deviated

- Acceptance asks the reader to stop setting `usage` and `edits`. Both remain
  required fields on `ObservedPlanningSession` and
  `src/core/report/planning-observed.ts` is outside this task's scope (task 3
  recorded the same conflict), so the reader keeps `usage:
  NULL_INTERACTIVE_USAGE` and derives `edits` from the turns. Nothing in the
  slicing path reads either once `turns` exists.
- The new fixture files were written even though only two paths are listed in
  `scope`; acceptance line 5 names `tests/fixtures/planning-codex/rollout.jsonl`
  and `source.json`, so they are required by the task itself.
- `tests/planning-observed-codex.test.ts` also gains a few turn assertions
  beyond the three changed groups; the preexisting assertions themselves are
  untouched.
