## Changed

- `src/watcher/service.ts` (new): exports exactly the Ports — `WATCH_ROLE_ENV`, `ServiceWorkerSpec`, `ServiceSupervisorOptions`, `ServiceSupervisorOverrides`, and `runServiceSupervisor` — and implements watcher-and-harness "Watch service supervisor". The supervisor spawns the watcher worker with the same Node executable, its `execArgv`, the `osq` entry, `watch` and the forwarded flags, `cwd` the project root, its env the supervisor's env with `OSQ_WATCH_ROLE=worker`, and its stdin ignored with stdout and stderr appended to `watch.log`. Before every spawn it rotates a `watch.log` larger than `watch.logMaxBytes` to `watch.log.1`, replacing any older one; its own lines are appended with an ISO timestamp. On a worker exit it removes `service.json` and exits 0 after a stop request, logs `new osq build; restarting the watcher` and respawns at once on `EXIT_NEW_BUILD` (imported from `service-build.ts`), and otherwise logs `watcher exited with <code or signal>; restarting in <n>s` and schedules a restart after `n` seconds. `n` starts at `watch.restartDelaySeconds`, doubles after each crash up to `watch.restartMaxDelaySeconds`, and resets after a worker that ran longer than the maximum or exited with `EXIT_NEW_BUILD`. On SIGTERM or SIGINT it sends SIGINT to a running worker and spawns no other; with no worker it cancels a pending restart, removes `service.json` through `removeWatchRecord` with its own pid, and exits 0. The supervisor never builds osq and never runs git.
- `tests/watch-supervisor.test.ts` (new): covers every scenario of "Watch service supervisor" with injected seams — an `EventEmitter` fake child whose `kill` records the signal, a fake timer list, a fake clock, and a temporary home and project. It asserts the built worker spec (command, args order, env role, cwd, log path) and checks the crash backoff table delay by delay (5s, 10s, 15s, 15s), the healthy-run reset (5s), the new-build immediate restart plus its log line, the stop while a worker runs (SIGINT, no respawn, exit 0, record removed), the stop during a restart delay (timer cancelled, no respawn, exit 0, record removed), and log rotation with replacement of an older `watch.log.1`.

Task verify `node --import tsx --import ./tests/git-test-env.ts --test tests/watch-supervisor.test.ts` passes (7 tests). `pnpm verify` (CLI and UI typechecks, build, all tests, lint) passes (3448 + 130 tests). `src/watcher/service.ts` is 243 lines, within the 250-line budget, and no file in `src/` holds the word "skipped".

## Deviated

The task named `runDevSupervisor` as the shape to follow. I implemented the state machine as a non-exported `ServiceSupervisor` class with small methods rather than one long function, because `tests/function-budget.test.ts` caps functions at 80 lines and a single closure-based supervisor body exceeded it. The public surface, injection seams, single `exit` path, and the promise that settles when the supervisor is done all match the requested shape.
